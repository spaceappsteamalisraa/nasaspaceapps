# Al Isra'a

An entry for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge, **"Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars."**

**Live site:** https://syd-47.github.io/nasaspaceapps/

The site tells the story of 20 pieces of NASA equipment left on the Moon and Mars, from Surveyor 1 to Ingenuity, and what NASA's adopted plans mean for each one.

- **Story:** turnable globes of the Moon and Mars with every object, its history, status and a live counter of how long it has been silent.
- **Dashboard:** filters, a map, a timeline from landing to silence, a sortable ledger and a "send a signal" demo using real light-travel times.
- **Prospective base sites:** NASA's nine candidate Artemis landing regions, and five human landing zones proposed at NASA's 2015 workshop, each with its nearest old equipment.
- **Citations and Sources:** every claim is linked to a NASA source.

The whole site is one self-contained file, `index.html`, with no build step.

Imagery: Apollo 11 site photo, NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University; Apollo 12 fly-over, NASA Scientific Visualization Studio; Perseverance rendering, NASA/JPL-Caltech. Other pictures are illustrations drawn for this site.

This is an independent project, not an official NASA publication.
